# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

La Famille Stick ("we", "us") publishes an animated web series on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. This policy explains how our internal publishing tool, La Famille Stick Publisher, handles data.

## Who we are

La Famille Stick is run by an individual non-professional publisher based in France. Contact: lafamillestick@gmail.com

## What the tool does

La Famille Stick Publisher is a private tool used only by us to upload our own original videos to our own accounts (YouTube channels La Famille Stick and Stick Family Survival, TikTok and Instagram accounts @lafamillestick and @stickfamilysurvival). It is not offered to the public and does not access any third-party user accounts.

## Data we access

When we connect our own accounts, the platforms give the tool an access token and basic account information (account ID, username, display name). The tool uses this only to confirm which of our accounts is connected and to publish videos and captions that we created. We may also read public statistics of our own videos (views, likes, comments) to plan future episodes, including counting A/B votes left in public comments.

## Data we do not collect

We do not collect, sell or share personal data about viewers. We do not use advertising trackers on this site. Comments read for audience votes are counted in aggregate and are not stored with personal identifiers.

## Storage and security

Access tokens are stored locally on our own computer, are never shared, and can be deleted at any time. We keep data only as long as needed to publish and measure our own content.

## Third-party services

The tool uses the YouTube API Services. By using our YouTube content you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Access granted to the tool can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. The tool also uses the TikTok Content Posting API and the Instagram API, subject to the TikTok Privacy Policy and the Meta Privacy Policy.

## Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct and delete personal data concerning you, and to object to its processing. To exercise these rights, email lafamillestick@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
